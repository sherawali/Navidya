# JNVST Class VI — Syllabus + PYQ research notes

Research date: 15 Nov 2026. Purpose: base for the mock test (`index.html`).

## 1. Exam for which we are building (JNVST 2027, session 2027-28)

- Exam date: **28 Nov 2026 (Saturday), 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM**, single phase.
- 80 objective questions, 100 marks, 2 hours, 4 options each (A/B/C/D), OMR.
- **1.25 marks per correct answer, no negative marking.**
- Divyang candidates: +40 minutes.
- Uttarakhand mediums: English, Hindi, Urdu.

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time | Minimum qualifying |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Mental Ability (Q1–20) + EVS (Q21–40) | 20 + 20 | 25 + 25 | 60 min | 14 marks (combined) |
| 2. Arithmetic (Q41–60) | 20 | 25 | 30 min | 7 marks |
| 3. Language (Q61–80) | 20 | 25 | 30 min | 7 marks |

Section timing is "suggested" — one booklet, candidate may move freely; a bell rings every 30 min.

### THE PATTERN CHANGED THIS YEAR
Up to JNVST 2026 (exam 13 Dec 2025): Mental Ability was 40 questions in 10 parts, no EVS.
From JNVST 2027: Mental Ability cut to 20 questions in 5 parts, and **EVS (20 questions) is new**.
So old papers match the new exam only partly. There are no real EVS previous-year questions.

## 2. Syllabus (from Prospectus 2027)

### Mental Ability — Q1–20, non-verbal, 5 parts × 4 questions
1. Pattern completion — **including alphabetical and number patterns** (new wording)
2. Figure series completion (3 figures given, pick the 4th)
3. Geometrical figure completion (triangle / square / circle)
4. Mirror **and water** imaging (prospectus sample uses a letter string, "TBAT" with mirror at XY)
5. Embedded (hidden) figure

Dropped compared with old papers: odd one out, figure matching, analogy, punched-hole / paper folding, space visualisation (cut-out pieces).

Prospectus numbering is inconsistent (Part III says Q9–11, Part IV says Q12–16); treat as 4 each.

### EVS — Q21–40: 15 standalone MCQs + 1 passage with 5 questions
- Natural world: transportation, rivers, mountains, plants and animals on land and water, natural disasters, types of houses/shelters, water cycle
- Human body: food and nutrients, hygiene and cleanliness, super senses, basics of digestive / circulatory / respiratory systems
- Science in daily life: food preservation, water and air pollution, conservation of water and soil
- Social surroundings: superlatives of India, states/capitals, national symbols, landscapes, festivals, seasons, forests, crops, clothes and fibres

Official sample questions: evaporation in the water cycle; dog's super sense of smell; preserving raw mango as pickle; Holi = festival of colours.
Official sample passage: "The Story of Ghadsisar" (King Ghadsi, Jaisalmer, 9 connected lakes, baolis) — this is from NCERT Class 5 EVS, so level = NCERT EVS Class 3–5. Passage questions are direct fact-lookup.

### Arithmetic — Q41–60
Prospectus says "12 topics" but lists these 9:
1. Number and numeric system — ascending, descending, nearest 10/100/1000, number names, value
2. Four fundamental operations on whole numbers
3. Factors and multiples and their properties
4. Fractions — add/subtract **like** fractions, and multiplication (unlike fractions and division of fractions NOT included)
5. Measurement of length, mass, capacity, time, money; unit conversion
6. Simplification of numerical expressions
7. Perimeter and area — perimeter of polygon; area of square, rectangle, triangle (as part of a rectangle)
8. Types of angles and simple applications (**including directions and mapping** — new)
9. Data analysis — bar diagram, **table and pictograph** (new)

No longer listed (were in the 2025/2026 list): decimals and operations on them, fraction↔decimal conversion, profit and loss.
Removed earlier (from 2024 onwards): percentage, simple interest, speed-distance-time, volume, average, approximation.

Emphasis: "understanding and application". Official examples: prime factorisation of 1000; average of first four odd numbers.

### Language — Q61–80
4 unseen passages × 5 questions. Pure reading comprehension.

## 3. Previous-year papers read (Arithmetic + Language text; MAT figures not visible)

| Year | Exam date | Arithmetic flavour |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 11 Aug 2021 | Old syllabus: SI, profit %, average, approximation, square root, HCF/LCM, decimals |
| 2022 | 30 Apr 2022 | Old syllabus: %, SI, volume, speed-time, mixed-fraction division, HCF/LCM, prime factorisation |
| 2023 | 29 Apr 2023 | Old syllabus: %, profit %, trains, volume, divisibility rules, HCF with prime powers |
| 2024 | (2024 paper) | New-style: number system, rounding to nearest 1000, primes, unit conversion, like fractions, perimeter/area, angle types, bar chart |
| 2025 | 18 Jan 2025 | New-style: prime factorisation, division algorithm, digit-formed numbers, mL→L, BODMAS with bar bracket, elapsed time, path area, like fractions, clock/spoke angles, bar graph, profit/loss in rupees |

The 2024 and 2025 papers are the closest to the 2027 syllabus; 2021–2023 arithmetic is mostly out of syllabus now.

### Arithmetic question types that keep repeating (still in syllabus)
- Greatest − smallest number formed from given digits (2023, 2025); greatest n-digit ± smallest m-digit (2021, 2024)
- Number names ↔ numerals (2021, 2023)
- Prime factorisation (2022, 2025 + prospectus example); prime-number facts (2021, 2024, 2025)
- Divisibility / common multiples; LCM "bells ring together" (2024); HCF (2021, 2022, 2023)
- Division: dividend = divisor × quotient + remainder (2025)
- BODMAS with nested brackets and vinculum (every year)
- Like-fraction addition/subtraction chains (2024, 2025); equivalent fractions (2024)
- Unit conversion: cm→km, m/cm, km/m, mL→L (2023, 2024, 2025)
- Clock/time: watch fast by n minutes, journey elapsed time (2023, 2025)
- Rectangle/square: equal-area, perimeter, tiles needed, path inside a park, shaded region, tables joined end to end
- Angles: reflex definition, angle type in a figure, clock hands, wheel spokes (2024, 2025)
- Bar graph: read values, compare, average (2024, 2025)
- Word problems: marks of three students, cost comparisons

Difficulty: mostly 1–2 step; 3–4 questions per paper need longer working.
Distractors are usually "one common mistake away" (wrong place value, forgot a bracket, wrong unit).

### Language pattern (all five years consistent)
- Passage length about 80–130 words; simple vocabulary with 2–3 harder words.
- Themes: nature and animals (spiders, golden eagle, snakes, autumn), science (volcano, robots, milk), values (honesty, determination, appearances), biography (Lal Bahadur Shastri, J.C. Bose, Helen Keller), small stories (picnic, pond, tug of war), books/health.
- Per passage, typically: 2–3 direct fact / fill-in-the-blank, 1 synonym or "means the same as", 1 antonym, sometimes 1 "which is NOT true", 1 main idea, and (2022–2024) a part-of-speech question ("'Deceptive' is a/an …").

### Old Mental Ability (2021–2026), for reference
10 parts × 4: odd one out, figure matching, pattern completion, figure series, analogy, geometrical figure completion, mirror image, punched-hole folding, space visualisation, embedded figure.
5 of these 10 carry over to 2027: pattern completion, figure series, geometrical figure completion, mirror image (now with water image), embedded figure.
